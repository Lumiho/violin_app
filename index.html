Getting the Best Detection

Mic Position
  • 2-4 feet from mic — too close overloads, too far picks up reverb
  • Aim the f-holes toward the mic
  • Phone on music stand facing you works well
Room & Noise
  • Soft furnishings reduce echo (carpet, curtains)
  • Close windows, turn off fans/AC
  • No other music or instruments playing
Playing Technique
  • Sustain notes 1-2 sec — detector needs stable pitch
  • Clean bow contact helps — scratchy tone confuses detection
  • One note at a time (double stops won't work)
If Detection Seems Off
  • Open G is hardest — play slightly louder on low notes
  • Check A4 calibration matches your tuner
  • Chrome/Edge work better than Safari
